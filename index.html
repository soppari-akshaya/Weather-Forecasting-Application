About This Application

Your simple and reliable weather companion for checking current weather and planning ahead.

Weather Forecasting Application

The Weather Forecasting Application helps users check current weather conditions for a selected location and view a detailed 10-day forecast.

Users can search for a city or use their current location to view temperature, humidity, wind speed, feels-like temperature, and upcoming weather conditions.
